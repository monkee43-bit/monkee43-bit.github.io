Privacy Policy — Lobster Sky Weather

Summary

Lobster Sky Weather shows NOAA weather, marine, tide, sun and moon information for coastal New England. There are no accounts, and we do not sell or share your personal information. An optional one-time Lobster Sky Plus upgrade ($0.99) is available through Google Play, and businesses can pay for optional map pins ($20 or $50 per month) through Stripe on a website, outside the app stores.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location only while the app is open (foreground). It never asks for background location. You can say no and pick a town from the list instead. Your location stays on your phone except for the lookups below: it is used only to look up the forecast and weather alerts, the nearest weather station (current conditions and barometer), nearest buoy, marine zone and tide station, to center the radar map, and, in Maine, to find your hunting Wildlife Management District.

What is sent to NOAA

To get forecasts and river levels, the app sends the latitude/longitude of the place you are viewing (rounded to about 4 decimal places) directly to U.S. government services: the National Weather Service (api.weather.gov), NOAA National Data Buoy Center (ndbc.noaa.gov), NOAA Tides & Currents (tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov), and the U.S. Geological Survey Water Services (waterservices.usgs.gov) for Maine stream gauges. On the Hunting tab (Maine only), the same latitude/longitude is sent to the Maine Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife’s public Wildlife Management District map (hosted on Esri ArcGIS Online, services1.arcgis.com) to find your district. The Radar screen and the Maine Pin Map download map tiles for the area on screen from USGS The National Map (basemap.nationalmap.gov) and radar images from NOAA (opengeo.ncep.noaa.gov) or, as a backup, Iowa State University’s Mesonet (mesonet.agron.iastate.edu); those requests reveal the map area you are viewing. Like any website, those services can see your device’s IP address. Their own privacy policies apply. We do not run our own server and never receive your location.

Stored on your phone

The last forecast for each place you view, your mute setting, saved places, theme preference, your business map listing and its small photo (if you make one), and (if you unlock it) Lobster Sky Plus entitlement state are saved on your phone only. You can clear saved forecasts in the app, or by clearing the app’s storage / uninstalling.

Purchases (Lobster Sky Plus)

The optional Lobster Sky Plus unlock is a one-time $0.99 Google Play purchase. Google processes the payment and keeps the purchase record. The app stores only a local “unlocked” flag (and, in development builds, a clearly labeled Dev unlock toggle). We never receive your payment card details.

Business listings and photos (Maine Pin Map)

Business owners can set up a map listing (name, category, town, address, what they serve, one photo and, on the Special pin, a daily special). In this version the listing and its photo are saved on your phone only and are not sent anywhere. The photo you pick is shrunk on your phone (about 320 pixels, under about 60 KB) before it is saved; only that small copy is kept, the app never reads other photos, and it does not use the camera. Android’s photo picker is used, so the app gets no access to the rest of your photo library. You can replace or remove the photo, or delete the listing, at any time. When public listings launch, the listing text and small photo will be uploaded to our listing service so other users can see them on the map; this policy will be updated before that happens. Pin subscriptions ($20 per month for a basic pin, $50 per month for a pin with a daily special) are paid on a website through Stripe, outside the app and the app stores. Stripe collects the business owner’s name, email and card details to process the payment under its own privacy policy; we receive only what we need to run the listing (business name, contact email and whether the subscription is active). We never see full card numbers. The app itself never takes payment for listings.

Sounds

Ambient sounds are audio files bundled inside the app and play on your device. The app never records or uses the microphone.

Accounts, tracking, ads and selling data

No sign-up or account. No analytics or tracking SDKs in this version. No ads. We do not sell, rent or trade any data.

Children

The app is a general-audience weather tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
